# Market Predictor – Brand Guidelines

## Brand Overview

**Market Predictor** is a bold, data-driven trading platform that empowers traders with machine learning predictions, technical analysis, and real-time market intelligence. Our brand voice is confident, urgent, and authoritative.

### Brand Promise
*Trade with conviction. Trade with data.*

### Core Brand Values
- **Accuracy**: Backed by ML, not guesswork
- **Speed**: Live signals, instant decisions
- **Authority**: Professional-grade analysis
- **Clarity**: No jargon, pure intelligence

## Logo & Wordmark

### Logo Files

| Format | File | Use Case |
|--------|------|----------|
| SVG | `logo.svg` | Web, digital (scalable) |
| PNG (1x) | `logo-1x.png` | Social media, favicons |
| PNG (2x) | `logo-2x.png` | High-DPI displays |
| PNG (3x) | `logo-3x.png` | Very high-DPI (future) |

### Logo Variations

#### Full Logo (Horizontal)
Icon (gradient square) + "Market Predictor" wordmark  
**Min size**: 120px wide  
**Spacing**: 12px between icon and text

#### Icon Only (Square)
Gradient square with candlestick chart  
**Min size**: 32px  
**Use when**: Favicon, app icon, social profiles, avatar

#### Wordmark Only (Text)
"Market Predictor" in Inter Bold  
**Min size**: 80px wide  
**Use when**: Narrow spaces, long-form design

#### Stacked (Vertical)
Icon centered above wordmark  
**Min size**: 100px wide  
**Use when**: Square spaces, mobile contexts

### Logo Colors

**Standard** (Dark backgrounds):
- Icon: `Linear gradient #FBBF24 → #10B981`
- Text: `#F9FAFB` (white)

**Reversed** (Light backgrounds):
- Icon: Same gradient (works on light)
- Text: `#0F172A` (navy)

**Monochrome** (Accessibility):
- Icon & Text: `#10B981` or `#0F172A`

### Logo Clearspace

Minimum clearspace around logo = logo height ÷ 4

```
[Clearspace] [Logo] [Clearspace]
     60px       240px      60px
```

### Logo Misuse (Don't Do This)

❌ Change colors or gradients  
❌ Rotate, skew, or distort  
❌ Add drop shadows or effects  
❌ Use on busy backgrounds (add white box if needed)  
❌ Scale disproportionately  
❌ Place too close to other logos

## Typography

### Font Families

| Font | Weight | Use | Fallback |
|------|--------|-----|----------|
| **Inter** | 400, 500, 600, 700 | Headlines, UI, body | -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, Segoe UI |
| **JetBrains Mono** | 400, 600 | Data, numbers, code | Courier New, monospace |

### Type Scale

```
H1 Display:  32px 700  (symbol names)
H2 Section:  24px 700  (page sections)
H3 Card:     18px 700  (card titles)
Body Large:  16px 400  (descriptions)
Body:        14px 400  (main text)
Body Small:  12px 500  (labels, UI)
Eyebrow:     11px 700  (uppercase labels)
Mono:        13px 400  (numbers, data)
```

### Hierarchy Rules

1. **Headlines are confident**: Bold, direct, no filler
   - ✅ "Market Predictor Signals"
   - ❌ "Our Powerful Market Predictor Signals"

2. **Data is monospace**: All numbers, prices, percentages
   - ✅ `67%` (monospace)
   - ❌ 67% (regular)

3. **Uppercase only for labels**:
   - ✅ "Model Confidence" (UI label)
   - ❌ "MODEL CONFIDENCE" (in prose)

4. **Sentence case for UI**:
   - ✅ "Analyze & Blend"
   - ❌ "Analyze & blend" or "ANALYZE & BLEND"

5. **Max line length 75 characters** for readability

### Font Pairing Examples

**Homepage Hero**
```
H1: Inter 56px 700
Subtitle: Inter 20px 400
Result: Bold, confident, modern
```

**Dashboard Metrics**
```
Label: Inter 11px 700 UPPERCASE
Value: JetBrains Mono 24px 600
Result: Data stands out, clear hierarchy
```

## Tone of Voice

### Brand Voice Keywords
**Confident | Direct | Technical | Authoritative | Urgent**

### Tone Examples

#### Headlines
| Bland | ✅ Market Predictor |
|-------|-------------------|
| "Our platform provides market predictions" | "Trade with conviction. Trade with data." |

#### CTAs
| Weak | ✅ Confident |
|------|------------|
| "Click here to learn more" | "Launch App" or "Analyze & Blend" |

#### Error Messages
| Apologetic | ✅ Direct |
|-----------|---------|
| "Sorry, we couldn't fetch the data. Please try again." | "Failed to load prices. Check your connection and try again." |

#### Feature Descriptions
| Sales-y | ✅ Precise |
|---------|----------|
| "Our amazing ML model gives you incredible predictions" | "HistGradientBoosting classifier with 20+ technical indicators" |

### Voice Guidelines

**Always:**
- Use active voice ("Signals update in real-time" not "Real-time updates are provided")
- Be specific ("67% confidence" not "high confidence")
- Assume trader knowledge (no over-explaining)
- Lead with action (verb-first buttons)

**Never:**
- Apologize or hedge ("Unfortunately" "probably" "might be")
- Use marketing fluff ("revolutionary" "cutting-edge")
- Assume weakness ("just" "only" "simply")
- Personalize too much (avoid "I" or "we" in UI)

### Writing Examples

**Good**: "Bullish. Model confidence 67%, news sentiment positive."  
**Bad**: "Our model kind of thinks the market might go up."

**Good**: "Paste earnings reports or breaking news here."  
**Bad**: "Please provide additional material information if desired."

**Good**: "Analyze & Blend"  
**Bad**: "Submit for Analysis"

- **Grid**: Subtle background grid (16px, 2% opacity) for data-heavy sections
- **Gradients**: Used sparingly on CTA buttons and hero sections

- Abstract trading-floor energy (optional)

- Real data, real screenshots, real examples

## Animation & Motion

### Transition Principles

**Micro-interactions:**
- Hover states: 0.2–0.3s ease-out
- Loading: Pulse or spinner (2–3s cycle)
- Signal updates: Subtle fade-in (0.3s)

**Accessibility:**
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` (remove animations for users)
- No auto-playing videos
- Avoid flashing (photosensitivity)

### Recommended Animations

```css
/* Hover button */
transition: all 0.3s ease-out;
transform: translateY(-2px);

/* Pulse dot (signal) */
animation: pulse 2s infinite;

/* Fade-in data */
animation: fadeIn 0.3s ease-out;
```

## Do's & Don'ts

### DO ✅

- Use the full brand color palette boldly
- Keep layouts clean and data-focused
- Use monospace for all numbers/prices
- Respect whitespace and breathing room
- Test contrast ratios (WCAG AA minimum)
- Use the brand voice consistently
- Assume trader audience knows markets

### DON'T ❌

- Use tints or custom colors (use the palette)
- Add unnecessary decorations
- Mix fonts (Inter + one monospace, that's it)
- Use placeholder copy (real examples instead)
- Apologize in error messages
- Over-explain market jargon
- Use "we" or "I" in UI copy
- Violate clearspace rules on logo
